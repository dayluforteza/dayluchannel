Questions

Asked about training.

Do you train groups?

Yes, small groups, adapting the content to the level of the whole group. We agree it before starting so the sessions make sense for everyone.

Is the training official or certified?

No. I do not issue official qualifications. It is practical training, focused on using your technology on your own.

Can I combine training and advice?

Yes, in fact they usually go together: first I review your situation and then we plan the sessions around what you really need.

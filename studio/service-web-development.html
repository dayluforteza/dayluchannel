Questions

Before you ask for a quote.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes, if you need to. I can deliver it with a simple editor and show you how to change the content that updates often.

Are domain and hosting included?

They can be part of the proposal. The cost depends on the provider and the expected traffic, and they are always in your name.

How long does it take?

It depends on the number of sections and on when texts and images are ready. The proposal shows the phases and the expected dates.

Do you guarantee first place on Google?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. You get the technical base and the content ready to rank with real work.

What does €1,000 include?

A landing page or a compact corporate site with structure, copy, design, responsive build, a working form, publishing and setup in your name. Larger sites get a fixed quote first.

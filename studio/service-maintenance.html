Questions

Asked about maintenance.

Can you check my computer remotely?

Many system and software reviews can be solved remotely. Physical faults, like a failing disk or memory, need a visit or leaving the computer with me.

Is preventive maintenance worth it?

For work machines, almost always. Checking the disk, updating and cleaning avoids stoppages that cost more than the review itself.

Will I know if it can be fixed before I pay?

Yes. I diagnose first and explain the scenario: repair, upgrade or replacement. You decide how to continue.

Method

Six stages. No mysterious steps.

You always know where your case is and what comes next. The scope and the price are clear before work starts.

01Listen
  1. 01

    Listen

    You tell me what is wrong or what you want to improve. I ask what I need to understand it.

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    I check the computer, network, system or website until the cause is clear.

  3. 03

    Propose

    Your options, their limits and my recommendation, with scope and price before we start.

  4. 04

    Resolve

    The agreed work, done with care for your equipment and your data.

  5. 05

    Verify

    We test it together and you get a written summary of what was done.

  6. 06

    Support

    If it makes sense, maintenance or improvements. If it does not, I will say so.

Hiring

From the first message to the close.

The first contact carries no commitment. If your case does not fit what I can offer, I will tell you clearly.

  1. 1

    You tell me your case

    By the form, WhatsApp or the solution finder, with whatever detail you have. You do not need the technical name of the problem.

  2. 2

    I ask for what is needed

    Only the essential data to understand the situation and its scope.

  3. 3

    I weigh the options

    I compare alternatives and drop what adds nothing to your case.

  4. 4

    Proposal and price

    You get the scope, the expected timing and the amount before starting.

  5. 5

    I carry out the work

    Under the agreed conditions, respecting your equipment and your data.

  6. 6

    We check the result

    Tests with me and a written summary of what was done.

  7. 7

    We see what else helps

    If it makes sense, improvements or maintenance. If not, I tell you just the same.

Questions

About working with me.

Does the first contact commit me to anything?

No. You tell me your case and I tell you whether I can help. If it does not fit what I offer, I say so clearly.

Why are there no public prices?

Each case has a different scope. You get a concrete proposal, with the amount and the timing, once I understand what is needed. Websites are the exception: they start at €1,000.

Do I need to know what is technically wrong?

No. Describe what happens in your own words. Finding the cause is my job.

Do you work on site or remotely?

Both. On site across Málaga and the metro area, by appointment, and remotely when the problem allows it.

What do I get at the end of a job?

A written summary of what was done, plus the documents of the handover: diagnosis, proposal, diagram or restore test, depending on the job.

01

Systems

Computers set up properly.

I review, configure and optimise your machines on Windows, Windows Server and macOS so they stay stable and fit the work you do.

Will I lose my files if the system is reinstalled?They should not be lost: the backup is part of the job. I check with you what to keep and verify it at the end.

Problems it solves

  • A new computer that still is not ready for daily work
  • A machine that struggles with a few tabs open
  • Data spread across folders, external disks and old accounts

What you get

  • Stable, updated machines adapted to how you work
  • Users and permissions so each person sees what they should
  • Data migrated and checked, nothing lost on the way

Includes

OS installationPeripheralsWindows ServerHardware upgradesShared resourcesRenewal advice

Who it is for

Home usersFreelancersOffices with several desksBusinesses that grew without order
02

Networks

A connection you can stop thinking about.

I install, organise and configure the network of your home, office or shop, from the internet line to every device.

Can I extend the Wi-Fi without building work?Often yes, with cabled access points where possible or a mesh network where not. I explain the limits of each.

Problems it solves

  • Wi-Fi that drops when several people connect
  • Unlabelled cables and cabinets nobody understands
  • Printers or card terminals that stop responding

What you get

  • Stable coverage with access points in the right place
  • Labelled, documented cabling: no more guessing
  • A base ready to add devices without starting over

Includes

CablingRouters and switchesAccess pointsLocal networksVPN for remote accessConnectivity diagnosis

Who it is for

Homes with poor coverageOffices setting upShops with POS and camerasBusinesses moving premises
03

Maintenance

Know what failed, and why.

When something breaks, getting it running again is half the job. The other half is understanding what happened so it does not return.

Will I know if it can be fixed before I pay?Yes. I diagnose first and explain the options: repair, upgrade or replacement. You decide.

Problems it solves

  • Computers that start slowly or freeze for no clear reason
  • Errors that come and go and nobody can reproduce
  • Overheating, noise or sudden shutdowns

What you get

  • A clear cause, not a blind diagnosis
  • Fewer repeat incidents thanks to preventive care
  • Early warning when a component starts to fail

Includes

Hardware and software diagnosisPreventive and corrective careOn-site and remote supportPerformance tuningWritten records

Who it is for

Home usersFreelancers who cannot stopShops with one critical tillOffices without IT staff
04

Security

Realistic protection.

Security starts with well-configured systems and good habits. I reduce the risk of losing data or access, without complicating your day.

Is the free antivirus enough?Often, if the system is updated and the habits are good. I check your use before recommending you pay for anything.

Problems it solves

  • The same password everywhere and accounts nobody controls
  • Backups that exist but were never restored
  • Shared folders open to everyone

What you get

  • Tested backups: you know you can recover
  • Less risk of losing data or account access
  • Clear criteria to tell a real warning from a scam

Includes

Security settings reviewUpdates and protectionPasswords and accessBackup setupPermissions review

Who it is for

People with photos and no copyFreelancers with client dataShops with POS terminalsOffices that cannot stop

What you get

  • A structure that tells visitors what to do next
  • Content written for people, not for machines

Use your own technology with confidence.

My background as a trainer lets me adapt every explanation to what you already know, from first steps to technical questions.

Scope

What I do. And what I don’t.

Knowing the limits is part of the service. If your case is outside them, you hear it from me on day one.

✓I do

  • Diagnose before touching anythingFirst the cause, then the fix.
  • Put scope and price in writingBefore any work starts, never after.
  • Back up your data before an installAnd check it is all there at the end.
  • Label and document every installationDiagram, labels and a written summary.
  • Test a restore of your backups with youA copy nobody has restored is not a backup.
  • Register domain and hosting in your nameYour website stays yours.

✕I don’t

  • Promise first place on GoogleBe wary of anyone who does.
  • Run advanced security audits or certificationsI point you to the right specialist instead.
  • Issue official qualificationsTraining is practical, not certified.
  • Sell you what you do not needEven when it means less work for me.
  • Replace hardware that an upgrade can saveYou get the numbers to decide.
  • Leave you depending on mePasswords, documents and access are handed over.

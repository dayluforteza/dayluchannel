Who I am

An IT technician who explains every step.

I am Daylu Forteza, an IT technician trained in network computer systems administration (ASIR) and microcomputer systems and networks (SMR).

Since 2017 I have combined technology with teaching, helping people understand and use it better. Since 2018 I have also worked in technical support, on site and remotely: repairs, incidents, networks and systems, including specialised equipment and software in the healthcare sector.

That taught me something I bring to every job: behind each technical problem there is a person who needs it solved. Knowing systems is half the work. The other half is listening, explaining and choosing the fix that still makes sense in a year.

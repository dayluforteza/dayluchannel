Questions

About working with me.

Does the first contact commit me to anything?

No. You tell me your case and I tell you whether I can help. If it does not fit what I offer, I say so clearly.

Why are there no public prices?

Each case has a different scope. You get a concrete proposal, with the amount and the timing, once I understand what is needed. Websites are the exception: they start at €1,000.

Do I need to know what is technically wrong?

No. Describe what happens in your own words. Finding the cause is my job.

Do you work on site or remotely?

Both. On site across Málaga and the metro area, by appointment, and remotely when the problem allows it.

What do I get at the end of a job?

A written summary of what was done, plus the documents of the handover: diagnosis, proposal, diagram or restore test, depending on the job.

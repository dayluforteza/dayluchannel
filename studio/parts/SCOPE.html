Scope

What I do. And what I don’t.

Knowing the limits is part of the service. If your case is outside them, you hear it from me on day one.

✓I do

  • Diagnose before touching anythingFirst the cause, then the fix.
  • Put scope and price in writingBefore any work starts, never after.
  • Back up your data before an installAnd check it is all there at the end.
  • Label and document every installationDiagram, labels and a written summary.
  • Test a restore of your backups with youA copy nobody has restored is not a backup.
  • Register domain and hosting in your nameYour website stays yours.

✕I don’t

  • Promise first place on GoogleBe wary of anyone who does.
  • Run advanced security audits or certificationsI point you to the right specialist instead.
  • Issue official qualificationsTraining is practical, not certified.
  • Sell you what you do not needEven when it means less work for me.
  • Replace hardware that an upgrade can saveYou get the numbers to decide.
  • Leave you depending on mePasswords, documents and access are handed over.

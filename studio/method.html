Questions

Asked before every job.

SystemsCan I keep the program I always use?

In most cases, yes. If it is old or depends on a specific system version, I tell you before starting so we can weigh alternatives.

NetworksDo I need to rewire my office?

Not always. I first check the quality of what is there. If the cabling holds the network, I adapt the configuration and equipment.

NetworksDo you leave documentation of the installation?

Yes. Labels, a network diagram and a summary of the configuration, so any future review starts from something clear.

MaintenanceCan you check my computer remotely?

Many system and software issues can be solved remotely. Physical faults, like a failing disk or memory, need a visit or a drop-off.

MaintenanceIs preventive maintenance worth it?

For work machines, almost always. Checking the disk, updating and cleaning avoids stoppages that cost more than the review.

SecurityWhere should I keep my backups?

At least two copies on different media, one of them outside the computer. The exact setup depends on your data and budget.

WebCan I update the website myself?

Yes, if you need to. I can deliver it with a simple editor and show you how to change the content that updates often.

WebHow long does a corporate website take?

It depends on the number of sections and on when texts and images are ready. The proposal shows the phases and the expected dates.

TrainingDo you train groups?

Yes, small groups, with the content adapted to the level of the whole group. We agree it before the first session.

Questions

Asked about security.

Do you do advanced security audits?

No. My work is basic, preventive security: settings, updates, permissions and copies. If your case needs a specialised audit or a certification, I tell you and point you to the right profile.

Where should I keep my backups?

The practical rule is at least two copies on different media, one of them outside the computer. The exact solution depends on your data and your budget.

Is the free antivirus enough?

It can be in many cases if the system is updated and the habits are good. I assess your use before recommending you pay for anything.

A well-designed, well-installed network makes work easier, improves connectivity and cuts daily problems. I install, organise and configure the infrastructure of your home, office or business, from the internet line to every device.

  • Every point tested before it is closed
  • Labels on both ends of each cable
  • Cabinet tidied, existing cabling reused where it holds

You keep

  • Test results per point

Questions

Asked about networks.

Do I need to rewire my office?

Not always. First I check the quality and state of what is there. If the cabling holds the network, I adapt the configuration and the equipment.

Can I extend the Wi-Fi without building work?

Often yes, with cabled access points where possible or a mesh network where not. I explain the advantages and limits of each option.

Do you leave documentation of the installation?

Yes. Labels, a network diagram and a summary of the configuration, so any future review starts from something clear.
